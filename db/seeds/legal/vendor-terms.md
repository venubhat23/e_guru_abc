## 1. Eligibility

Vendors must hold a valid GSTIN and PAN, and an FSSAI licence if supplying any food items. You must complete KYC and be approved before receiving assignments.

## 2. Offers

You may offer to supply products and packages from our master catalogue by setting your price, minimum order quantity, weekly capacity, lead time, advance percentage and balance terms. **Changes to an offer apply only to new bookings.**

## 3. Assignments and SLA

- Assignments must be accepted or rejected within the SLA (default 24 hours). Expired or rejected assignments affect your vendor score.
- Once accepted, you must produce, quality-check (with photos), dispatch with AWB per recipient, and upload proof of delivery.

## 4. Confidentiality

Recipient data is shared only for assignments you accept and must be used **solely** to fulfil them. You must not contact recipients or clients directly for any other purpose, nor retain their data after delivery.

## 5. Payouts

- An **advance** (your advance %) becomes due when you accept an assignment.
- The **balance** is due on delivery or after your credit days, less any approved penalties.
- Payouts are paid by bank transfer with a UTR reference. You must upload your own tax invoice and mark each payout as received.
- Payouts may be withheld for bookings under dispute until resolved.

## 6. Quality and penalties

Late delivery, wrong items or quality failures may result in penalties, replacement at your cost, or suspension.

## 7. Termination

Either party may end the relationship with 30 days' notice. We may suspend immediately for fraud, data misuse or repeated SLA breaches.
