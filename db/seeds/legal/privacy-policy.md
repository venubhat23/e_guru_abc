## 1. Who we are

Avighna eGiftMitra ("we", "us") operates a corporate-gifting platform in India. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share and protect personal data, in line with the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023** ("DPDP Act") and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and rules made under it.

## 2. Data we collect

**From website visitors and enquiries**
- Name, work email, phone number, company, employee count, occasion, budget and message submitted via the Contact Us form.
- Technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, used for security and analytics.

**From client companies (HR users)**
- Account details of HR users: name, email, phone, role.
- Company details: legal name, GSTIN, PAN, billing and shipping addresses.

**Employee (recipient) data provided by clients**
- Employee code, name, email, phone, department, designation, location, delivery address, pincode, state, T-shirt size, date of birth and joining date (only where needed for birthday, anniversary or onboarding gifting).

**From vendors**
- Business details, GSTIN, PAN, FSSAI licence, bank account details (stored encrypted), warehouse address and KYC documents.

## 3. Why we use it

- To respond to enquiries and provide demos and quotes.
- To fulfil bookings: personalise gifts, pack, ship and track deliveries.
- To issue invoices, process payments and payouts, and meet tax and accounting obligations.
- To verify vendors and prevent fraud.
- To send service emails and notifications. We send marketing only with your consent and you can opt out anytime.

## 4. Legal basis

We process personal data on the basis of **consent** (e.g. Contact Us form), for the **performance of a contract** with our clients and vendors, and for **legitimate uses** permitted under the DPDP Act, including compliance with law. For employee data, our client company is the Data Fiduciary and we act as its **Data Processor**.

## 5. Who we share it with

- **Vendors** receive only the recipient details needed to fulfil the specific assignment they have accepted — never your full employee directory.
- **Courier partners** receive names, addresses and phone numbers for delivery.
- **Payment partners** (e.g. Razorpay) process payment information. We do not store card details.
- **Service providers** for hosting, email and storage, under confidentiality obligations.
- Authorities, where required by law.

We **do not sell** personal data.

## 6. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes above. Invoices and related records are retained for at least 8 years as required by GST law. Employee delivery data is deleted or anonymised within 12 months of booking completion unless the client asks otherwise.

## 7. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), encryption at rest for sensitive fields such as bank details, role-based access controls, tenant isolation between companies and vendors, and audit logs of changes.

## 8. Your rights

Subject to the DPDP Act, you may request access to, correction of, or erasure of your personal data, withdraw consent, and nominate another person to exercise your rights. Employees should contact their employer first; we will assist our clients in responding.
