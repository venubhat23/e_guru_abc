## 1. Cancellation by the client

| When you cancel | What happens |
|---|---|
| Before the vendor confirms the booking | **Full refund** issued as a credit note, and money returned to the original payment method within 5–7 business days |
| After vendor confirmation, before production starts | Refund minus any non-recoverable costs (e.g. branded material already ordered), up to 25% of the booking value |
| After production has started | Cancellation not possible for personalised or branded items; non-branded items may be partially refunded at our discretion |

To cancel, use **Cancel booking** on the booking page in your HR portal, or email support.

## 2. Damaged, defective or wrong items

- Raise an issue against the affected recipient from the booking page **within 7 days** of delivery, with photos where possible.
- We will **replace** the item at no cost, or issue a **credit note** for the affected kit if replacement is not possible.

## 3. Failed or returned deliveries

- If a delivery fails because of an incorrect or incomplete address, phone number or recipient unavailability after three attempts, we will contact you to arrange re-delivery. Re-delivery charges may apply.
- Perishable items (e.g. sweets, chocolates) that are returned cannot be re-sent and are not refundable.

## 4. Refund method and timelines

- Online payments are refunded to the original payment method within **5–7 business days** of approval.
- Bank-transfer payments are refunded to the paying company's bank account within **7 business days**.
- Every refund is accompanied by a **GST credit note**.

## 5. Contact

For cancellation or refund queries, email **support@egiftguru.in** with your booking number.
