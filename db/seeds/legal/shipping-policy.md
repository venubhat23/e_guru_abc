## Delivery coverage

We deliver to serviceable pincodes across India through our vendor network and courier partners. Coverage for each booking is confirmed when it is assigned.

## Delivery modes

- **Individual home delivery** — each employee receives their gift at home.
- **Bulk to office** — all kits are delivered to one or more office addresses you choose.
- **Employee self-fill** — employees enter their own address and size through a secure link.

## Timelines

Each package shows a lead time. Typical delivery is 5–10 business days after confirmation for metros and 7–14 business days for other locations. Festive peaks may extend timelines; book early.

## Tracking

Every recipient gets a courier name and AWB number once dispatched. You can track status per recipient from your booking page and download a delivery report.

## Delivery charges

Delivery charges, if any, are shown in your quote before you submit the booking.
